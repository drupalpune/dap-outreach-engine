# Host ICP — who to target and why

The venue ask is small: one room, one Saturday morning, twenty-odd people. The
filter is not size or budget, it is **whether someone inside the org already
cares about developer community**.

## Strong fit

- **Product engineering companies, 50–500 people, with a PHP/Drupal/open-source
  footprint.** They have a cafeteria or town-hall space, weekend access is a
  solved problem, and an engineering manager can approve it without a committee.
- **Digital agencies and services firms that build on Drupal or WordPress.**
  Hosting is the cheapest developer-brand spend available to them.
- **Coworking spaces in Baner / Hinjewadi / Kharadi / Viman Nagar.** Community
  programming is literally their product. Often the fastest yes.
- **Orgs that have hosted another tech community** — PyDelhi-style chapters,
  WordPress, JS, DevOps meetups. Precedent means the internal process exists.

## Workable, but slower

- **Large enterprises and GCCs.** Great rooms, real security and facilities
  process. Start six weeks out, not four. Needs an internal champion.
- **Engineering colleges.** Space is easy, weekend access and the audience mix
  are harder. Good for a larger one-off, less good for the monthly rhythm.

## Usually not a fit

- Anyone who asks what the sponsorship package costs — this is not a sponsorship sale
- Orgs with no weekend building access at all
- Pure sales/marketing offices with no engineers in the building
- Anywhere over 90 minutes from the city centre; attendance falls off a cliff

## Who inside the org

Ranked by how often they can just say yes:

1. **Community manager / DevRel** — this is their job. Best first contact where one exists.
2. **Engineering head / VP Engineering / CTO** — can approve, and cares about the hiring signal.
3. **Engineering manager who is personally in the community** — the warmest door. If you have met someone at a conference, start here regardless of title.
4. **HR / L&D / employer branding** — will say yes for the hiring and brand value, but may need engineering to co-sign.
5. **Admin / facilities** — the wrong first contact. They own the room but not the decision. You will get routed here eventually; do not start here.

## Sourcing signals worth checking

- Careers page listing Drupal, PHP, or CMS roles
- An engineering blog with named authors — those authors are also speaker leads
- Past sponsor or host of any Pune tech event
- Drupal.org organisation profile, or staff with Drupal.org profiles
- Someone from the org has attended a DAP meetup before — check past RSVPs first

## Volume discipline

Eight to ten researched first-touches per cycle. Not thirty. This is a small
pool you will keep running into for years; a burnt list does not grow back.
See section 10 of the build plan.
