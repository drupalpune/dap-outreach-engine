# What a DAP meetup is

## One-paragraph description

Drupalers Association Pune (DAP) is the Pune chapter of the global Drupal
community. We run a free, in-person meetup on the third Saturday of every month
— talks, a lightning slot, and an open discussion. Attendees are mostly working
developers, tech leads and engineering managers from Pune product and services
companies, plus students and freelancers. Drupal is the anchor, but sessions
range across PHP, DevOps, AI tooling and open source generally.

## The shape of a meetup

| | |
|---|---|
| Day | Third Saturday of the month |
| Time | 10:00 – 13:30 (arrival from 09:30) |
| Attendance | 25–40, RSVPs run higher than turnout |
| Cost | Free to attend, no ticketing |

Running order:

1. **09:30** Arrival, tea, name tags
2. **10:00** Welcome + community updates (10 min)
3. **10:15** Main session — 45 min + 15 min Q&A
4. **11:15** Break
5. **11:30** Lightning talk — 25 min + 5 min Q&A
6. **12:00** BoF — open discussion, no slides
7. **12:45** Wrap, group photo, informal chat
8. **13:30** Out of the room

## What we ask a host for

- A room seating **20–30**, for roughly **09:30–13:30** on the Saturday
- A **projector or large screen** with HDMI
- **Wi-Fi** for the speakers
- **Entry access** for a named list on a weekend
- Optional: tea/coffee, or snacks. Nice, not required — we have covered this ourselves before.

## What a host gets

- Their team walks downstairs to a technical event; no travel, no ticket
- Logo and name on the LinkedIn announcement, the recap post, and the meetup.com listing
- A verbal thank-you and a slide at the top of the session
- 25–40 engineers in their building for a morning — which is a hiring signal,
  whether or not anyone says the word hiring
- A standing invitation for their engineers to speak at a future meetup

## What we handle

Speakers, agenda, promotion, RSVPs, name tags, photos, and the recap post. The
host provides the room; we run the event. We clear the room by the agreed time
and leave it as we found it.
